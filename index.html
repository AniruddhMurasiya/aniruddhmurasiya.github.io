MATLAB/Simulink · PSS®E · HVDC Control

ANN vs PID Control for VSC-HVDC Networks

Compared ANN and PID controller response for a two-terminal VSC-HVDC transmission network under operating changes and fault disturbance conditions.

  • Modelled VSC-HVDC control behaviour and trained ANN logic using PID response data.
  • Achieved approximately 42% lower voltage overshoot and 35% faster settling time in simulation.
  • Documented results through structured technical reporting and engineering recommendations.
